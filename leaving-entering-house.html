Doa Keluar Rumah

A du'a for leaving the home while placing your trust in Allah and seeking His protection.

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللّٰهِ، لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللّٰهِ

Bismillahi, tawakkaltu 'alallah, la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah.
Indonesian

Dengan nama Allah, aku bertawakal kepada Allah. Tidak ada daya dan kekuatan kecuali dengan pertolongan Allah.

English

In the name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah. There is no power and no strength except by Allah.

Makna Doa Keluar Rumah

Leaving the home is not a small act. A believer steps out with tawakkul, knowing that safety, guidance, and strength come only from Allah. This du'a reminds the heart that no movement has real power except by His will.

It is a quiet declaration of dependence: I begin with Allah, I rely on Allah, and I move only by Allah’s permission.

Doa Masuk Rumah

A du'a for entering the home while asking Allah for goodness, blessing, and trust in Him.

اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّيْ أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَ الْمَوْلِجِ وَخَيْرَ الْمَخْرَجِ بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ وَلَجْنَا وَبِسْمِ اللّٰهِ خَرَجْنَا وَعَلَى اللّٰهِ رَبِّنَا تَوَكَّلْنَا

Allahumma innii as-aluka khayral mawlaji wa khayral makhraj. Bismillahi walajnaa, wa bismillahi kharajnaa, wa 'alallahi rabbina tawakkalnaa.
Indonesian

Ya Allah, sesungguhnya aku memohon kepada-Mu kebaikan tempat masuk dan kebaikan tempat keluar. Dengan menyebut nama Allah kami masuk, dengan menyebut nama Allah kami keluar, dan hanya kepada Allah, Tuhan kami, kami bertawakal.

English

O Allah, I ask You for the goodness of entering and the goodness of leaving. In the name of Allah we enter, in the name of Allah we leave, and upon Allah, our Lord, we rely.

Makna Doa Masuk Rumah

Entering the home is a moment to bring barakah into the space. This du'a asks Allah for goodness in both entry and exit, and it teaches the heart to remain attached to Him in every transition.

The believer does not enter relying on himself. He enters with Allah’s name, with trust in Allah, and with a heart that knows blessing comes only from Him.
