Doa Sebelum Tidur

A short du'a before sleeping, surrendering life and death to Allah.

بِسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَحْيَا وَبِاسْمِكَ أَمُوتُ

Bismika allahumma ahyaa wa bismika amuut.
Indonesian

Dengan nama-Mu ya Allah, aku hidup dan dengan nama-Mu aku mati.

English

In Your name O Allah, I live and in Your name I die.

Makna Doa Sebelum Tidur

Sleep is a reminder of temporary death. The soul leaves the body by Allah’s permission, and we surrender ourselves completely to Him before closing our eyes.

This du'a builds awareness that life and death are not controlled by us, but by Allah alone.

Doa Bangun Tidur

A du'a upon waking, thanking Allah for giving life after temporary death.

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ

Alhamdulillaahilladzii ahyaanaa ba'da maa amaatanaa wa ilaihin nusyuur.
Indonesian

Segala puji bagi Allah yang telah menghidupkan kami setelah mematikan kami, dan kepada-Nya kami kembali.

English

Praise be to Allah Who gave us life after causing us to die, and to Him is the return.

Makna Doa Bangun Tidur

Waking up is not guaranteed. It is a return of the soul by Allah’s mercy. This du'a trains the heart to recognize every morning as a second chance.

Life is renewed not by your control, but by Allah’s will alone.
