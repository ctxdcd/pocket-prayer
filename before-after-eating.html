Doa Sebelum Makan

A short remembrance before eating, asking Allah for blessing and protection.

اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ لَنَا فِيمَا رَزَقْتَنَا وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Allahumma baarik lanaa fiimaa razaqtanaa wa qinaa 'adzaaban-naar.
Indonesian

Ya Allah, berkahilah rezeki yang Engkau berikan kepada kami, dan peliharalah kami dari siksa api neraka.

English

O Allah, bless for us the provision You have given us, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.


Jika lupa membaca doa di awal: Bismillahi awwaluhu wa akhiruhu.
Makna Doa Sebelum Makan

This du'a teaches gratitude before eating. Food is not ours by right; it is a provision from Allah. We ask Him to place barakah in it and to protect us from the Fire, because every blessing should pull the heart back to Him.

Eating becomes an act of remembrance, not just routine. A believer does not eat carelessly; he begins with awareness and ends with gratitude.

Doa Sesudah Makan

A humble expression of praise after being fed, watered, and sustained by Allah.

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنَا وَسَقَانَا وَجَعَلَنَا مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ

Alhamdulillahilladzi ath'amanaa wa saqoonaa wa ja'alanaa minal muslimiin.
Indonesian

Segala puji bagi Allah yang telah memberi kami makan dan minum, serta menjadikan kami termasuk golongan orang-orang muslim.

English

All praise is for Allah Who fed us, gave us drink, and made us among the Muslims.

Makna Doa Sesudah Makan

After eating, the believer returns praise to Allah. Food, drink, and even the ability to enjoy them are from Him alone. This du'a keeps the heart from arrogance and keeps gratitude alive.

Being made among the Muslims is itself a great blessing. So this du'a gathers both worldly provision and the highest gift: guidance.
